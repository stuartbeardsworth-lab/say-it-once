Say It Once

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 September 2026.

Say It Once helps people recovering from a serious injury or illness keep a record of what happened, how it affects them, and their appointments, treatment, costs, letters and contacts. This page explains what happens to that information. The short version: your record stays on your device. We never receive it.

What Say It Once keeps, and where

Everything you write or add (entries, notes, photos, letters and settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's storage (the browser's storage, if you use Say It Once as a website). It is not sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no sign-in and no server holding your record.

What we collect

Nothing. In particular, Say It Once:

  • has no analytics, tracking or advertising;
  • uses no cookies;
  • loads no third-party scripts or fonts;
  • doesn't collect your name, email address, location or contacts.

The app is published through Netlify, a web hosting company. Like any website host, Netlify's servers receive your device's internet address and basic browser details when the app is downloaded or updated. This never includes anything from your record. See Netlify's privacy policy.

When information leaves your device

Only when you choose to send it:

  • Reports. When you make a Summary, Evidence Pack or Full Record, it is made on your device. It goes only where you choose to share or save it, for example by email to a solicitor. Entries you marked "Keep this private" are never included.
  • Backups. A backup you save holds your whole record, private entries included, so that it can be restored. It is not encrypted. Keep it somewhere safe, such as your own cloud drive.
  • Calendar and contacts. If you add an appointment to your calendar or export contacts, that file goes to the app you choose.

Once shared, a copy is looked after by whoever you sent it to, under their own privacy rules.

Photos and the camera

If you add a photo, Say It Once asks your device for the camera or your photo library at that moment. The photo is saved only in your record on the device.

Listen

Listen reads the screen aloud using your device's own text-to-speech. Say It Once chooses voices your device says are built in, and doesn't send the words anywhere itself.

Keeping your record safe

Anyone who can open your device and the app can read your record, so keep your device locked. If the device is lost, the app is uninstalled, or the app's or browser's data is cleared, the record is deleted with it. Saving a backup now and then is the only way to get it back.

Deleting your record

You can delete one record in My records, or everything in Privacy & backup. Deleting removes it from the device completely. Backups and reports you have already shared or saved elsewhere are not affected.

Health information

Say It Once records and organises what you tell it. It doesn't give medical, legal or benefits advice, and it doesn't assess or score your health.

Children

Say It Once is meant for adults.

Changes to this policy

If Say It Once ever starts to send any information off your device (for example an optional way to use your record on more than one device), this policy will be updated first and the app will ask before anything is sent.

Contact

Say It Once is made by an independent developer in the UK. You can reach them through the contact details on the app's Google Play listing.
