## Trying Say It Once

Thank you for trying Say It Once. It's a way to keep a record of what
happened after a serious injury or illness, how it affects you, and your
appointments, treatment, costs, letters and contacts, so you can share the
right parts with a solicitor, employer, the DWP or a doctor without
explaining everything again.

This is an early test version. It will change as people tell us what works
and what doesn't.

**Open it:** https://say-it-once-home.netlify.app

**Put it on your phone:** on Home, tap **See how, and one thing to know
first** in the **Keep Say It Once on your phone** box (or, if you've put
that box away, **Add to phone** at the bottom of Home), and follow the steps
for your phone. On an iPhone, do this before you start
writing, because the home-screen version keeps its own separate record
(you can move a record across with a backup, see below).

**Try it without your own details:** open **Help** (at the bottom of Home)
and press **Load the example record**. It adds a made-up record you can delete afterwards.

**Keep a backup:** in **Privacy & backup**, press **Make a backup** now and
then, and keep the file somewhere safe, such as your email. The backup is
not locked with a password, so keep it private.

**Your privacy:** your record stays on your phone or computer. Nothing is
sent to us or anyone else, and nothing is tracked. We can't see what you
write, and we can't recover it if it's lost, which is why backups matter.

**Tell us what you think:** [how to contact you: fill this in]. It helps
most to hear what was confusing, what didn't work, and what you expected
to be there. Please don't send us real details from your record.

## Known limits in this version

- **It lives on one device.** There are no accounts and no syncing yet. On
  an iPhone, Safari and the home-screen app keep separate records; move a
  record between them with a backup.
- **Safari can clear it.** If you use it in Safari without adding it to the
  home screen, Safari may clear its data after about a week without a
  visit. Adding it to the home screen, or keeping backups, avoids this.
- **Backups aren't encrypted.** Anyone with the backup file can read it,
  including entries marked private.
- **Very large backups.** A backup is opened all at once, so one of several
  hundred megabytes may not restore on an older phone.
- **Files** can be up to 25 MB each.
- **PDFs aren't set up for screen readers.** The zip file includes a web
  page copy of the report that works well with them.
- **If making a PDF fails** because the app couldn't load its PDF tools,
  close Say It Once completely and open it again.
- **Listen** in the header reads the screen aloud. There's no dictation:
  use the microphone on your phone's keyboard.
- **Deleting** removes an entry from your device, but can't take back a PDF,
  zip or backup you've already shared or saved.
- **Support contacts** in Find support are being checked before a wider
  release.
