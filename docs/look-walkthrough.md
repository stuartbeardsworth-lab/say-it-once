# Look and feel walkthrough

The app now looks like the original: the speech-bubble logo with the orange
dot, navy, cream and orange, the Manrope and Inter fonts, and Home's cards.
Nothing about how it works has changed.

Open the Deploy Preview on your iPhone, and on a computer if you can.

## Looks right

1. [ ] The navy header has the speech-bubble logo with the orange dot,
   **Say It Once**, and **No need to relive it.** underneath.
2. [ ] Home starts with **After an injury, accident or illness…** and
   **Keep everything together, so you don't have to start again.**
3. [ ] Under **What do you need today?** are cards for **Add something**
   (with an orange +), **Quick Note**, **Find in my record** and
   **Use my record**, then a peach **Find support** card.
4. [ ] **Your record** shows cards for What happened, How it affects me and
   Keep track.
5. [ ] Near the bottom is a box with How to use, Questions and answers, Add
   to phone and Privacy & backup, and the footer ends with
   **Record it → Keep it together → Use it when you need it**.
6. [ ] The headings use the rounder Manrope font, like the old app. This
   works with Wi-Fi off too, because the fonts are part of the app.
7. [ ] Add Say It Once to your home screen again (delete the old icon
   first). The icon is the speech bubble on navy.

## Still easy to use

8. [ ] On a computer, press Tab a few times on Home. Each card gets a thick
   navy outline with an orange edge, and Enter opens it.
9. [ ] Set **Text size** to Largest. Everything, including the cards and the
   header, gets bigger, and nothing is cut off.
10. [ ] With VoiceOver on, swipe to the Add something card. It says "Add
    something, button", then the line under it.

## What's deliberately different from the old app

- Orange words (like **Keep it together**) use a slightly deeper orange,
  because the original orange is too pale to read as text. The logo dot and
  the icons keep the original orange.
- There's no **Listen** button and no dictation, as decided earlier.
- The card wording says only what the new app does. For example, Quick Note
  says "Write a few words or add a photo", with no dictation.
