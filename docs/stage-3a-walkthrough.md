# Stage 3a walkthrough: Home, Quick Notes, My records, What happened

The same steps run automatically on every push in `e2e/stage3a.spec.ts`, but
please try them once yourself, ideally on your iPhone. Use the Deploy
Preview link on the pull request. Tick each box as you go.

## Quick Notes

1. [ ] On Home, tap **Quick Note**. Write a sentence and tap **Save as Quick
   Note**. The note appears under **A Quick Note to file**, marked "Not filed yet". Once filed, it leaves Home.
2. [ ] Tap **Quick Note** again, write something, then tap **Cancel**. You're
   asked "This hasn’t been saved". Tap **Keep editing**: your words are still
   there. Tap **Cancel** again, then **Discard this note**.
3. [ ] Write another note and tap **Save and file it now**. Choose **How it
   affects me**, then an area such as **Walking and moving around**, then
   **File note**. The note now says where it's filed.
4. [ ] Write a note, tap **Add a photo** and take or choose a photo. File it
   in **Letters & documents**. Reload: the photo is still there. (It has also
   been kept as a document, which you'll see in Stage 3b.)
5. [ ] Tap **See all Quick Notes**. You can **Edit** a note, **Change where
   it’s filed**, and **Delete** it (you're asked first, with focus on Cancel).

## What happened

6. [ ] From the footer, open **What happened**. Type in "What happened?" and
   stop. "Saved" appears. Open **Other details** and fill in one box.
7. [ ] Reload. Everything you typed is still there.
8. [ ] File a Quick Note in **What happened**. It appears at the bottom of the
   What happened screen under **Quick Notes filed here**.

## My records

9. [ ] Open **My records**. Tap **Add another record** and call it something
   like "Fall at work, March 2026". It becomes the one being shown, and every
   screen now shows "Record: Fall at work, March 2026".
10. [ ] Open **Quick Notes**: it's empty, because this record is new. Go back
    to **My records**, tap **Show this record** on the first one, and your
    notes are back.
11. [ ] **Rename** a record. **Delete** the one you added (you're asked
    first). When only one record is left, there's no Delete button.
12. [ ] Type your name in **Your name**, reload, and it's still there.

## Accessibility

13. [ ] With VoiceOver on, open a Quick Note, then swipe to **Keep this
    private**. It's read as a checkbox, with its explanation.
14. [ ] At **Largest** text size on the iPhone, the What happened screen fits
    without sideways scrolling.
