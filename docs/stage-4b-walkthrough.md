# Stage 4b walkthrough: Use my record, step by step

The same checks run automatically on every push (`e2e/stage4b.spec.ts`,
`e2e/stage4a.spec.ts` and the privacy property tests). Please try it yourself
on the Deploy Preview. Start by loading the example record: footer,
**Building blocks (for review)**, **Try the reports**, **Load the example
record**.

## The steps

1. [ ] **Use my record** asks **Who is it for?** Tap **Continue** without
   choosing: it says "Choose who it’s for." Choose **A solicitor** and
   **Continue**.
2. [ ] **What do they need?** lists only the solicitor's options. **Back**
   returns to the first question with your answer still chosen.
3. [ ] Choose **A personal injury claim** and **Continue**. **What goes in**
   lists each section with a tick box, how many entries are in it, and what
   is suggested (for example "The newest 5"). Sections with nothing in
   them say **Nothing recorded yet.**
4. [ ] Tap **8 private entries are not included**. It opens to show how many
   of each kind were left out (for example "1 letter or document"), never
   what they say.

## Choosing what goes in

5. [ ] Go **Back** twice, choose **Just for me**, then **Everything in my
   record**. Untick **Contacts**.
6. [ ] Next to **Appointments**, tap **Choose entries**. A window lists the
   three appointments that aren't private. Tap **Choose none**, tick
   **Physiotherapy**, then **Use these**. It now says "1 of 3 entries".
7. [ ] Tap **Create the report**. The report has no Contacts section and only
   the physiotherapy appointment.
8. [ ] **Change what’s included** takes you back with your choices kept.

## From Find

9. [ ] Go to **Find in my record** and search for **fracture clinic**. Tap
   **Use these results in a report**.
10. [ ] **Use my record** says "Using X of your Y search results". If any
    results were private, it says they won't be included.
11. [ ] Choose **Just for me**, **Everything in my record**. Only the
    sections with matching entries are ticked, and the report contains
    just those entries.

## Keyboard and screen reader

12. [ ] Do steps 1 to 7 with only the keyboard: Tab, Shift+Tab, arrow keys
    in the choices, Space to tick, Enter to press buttons. On each step,
    focus moves to the new heading. In **Choose entries**, Escape closes
    the window and focus returns to its button.
13. [ ] With VoiceOver on the iPhone, each step's heading is read out when
    you move on.
